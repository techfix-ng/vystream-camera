# VyStream Android 2.6.4 — crew intercom

- Accepts Director Talkback (`VYSTB1`) and crew-relayed talkback (`VYSTB2`).
- Displays the permanent saved name of the camera operator currently speaking.
- Rejects packets not carrying one of the phone's paired tokens.
- Keeps intercom audio outside the encoded livestream and MP4 path.
- Compatible with VyStream OBS Plugin 2.7.1 on Windows and macOS.
